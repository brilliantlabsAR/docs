# Deleting your Brilliant Noa account 

To delete your Brilliant Noa account, click on the **NOA** title to bring up the menu. Scroll down to **Account Settings** and tap on it to expand the section.

<div style="text-align:center">
    <img src="/brilliant-noa/images/account-settings.png" alt="How to open account settings" width="50%"/>
</div>

Tap **Delete Account** and confirm. Your account will then be deleted after 30 days. If you log back within 30 days, your account will be restored.

<div style="text-align:center">
    <img src="/brilliant-noa/images/delete-account.png" alt="How to delete the Brilliant Noa user account" width="50%"/>
</div>
